## 1. Established Canon Locks (From Production Architect Brief)

1. **Setting and Time Lock (`CANON`)**
   - The entirety of Case 001 takes place in **Harlem, New York City, in 2026**.
   - **Prohibition**: Do not introduce scenes set in Jamaica.
   - **Universe Boundary**: *The Miss Celia Chronicles* occupy a separate, earlier period of the wider DUST universe and must not be merged into Case 001 scenes.

2. **Mister Grant Lock (`CANON`)**
   - American widower.
   - His marriage lasted **less than two years**.
   - He is dealing with the aftermath of his wife's death.
   - **Prohibition**: Do not invent additional biography, appearance details, occupation, or personal history as canon unless confirmed in source records.

3. **Wrasal Lock (`CANON`)**
   - Apprentice working with experienced practitioners.
   - Quick learner.
   - Speaks **composed standard British English**.
   - **Visual Lock (`REFERENCE NEEDED`)**: Visual appearance must follow approved character references when available. Do not substitute a generic occult investigator appearance.

4. **The Practitioners Lock (`CANON`)**
   - **The Keeper**, **The Examiner**, and **The Builder** belong to the practitioner framework around the case.
   - **Prohibition**: Preserve their distinctions. Do not collapse them into one character or assign dialogue, actions, or visual appearances without checking the screenplay and character records (`REFERENCE NEEDED`).

5. **The Deceased Wife & Astral Mental Chamber Lock (`CANON`)**
   - A figure that appears to be the deceased wife **independently appears in the astral mental chamber of each practitioner**.
   - Her true identity and nature are **not confirmed**.
   - Familiarity does **not** prove identity.
   - **Prohibition**: Visual prompts and shot plans must preserve this uncertainty — do not depict her automatically as a conventional transparent ghost, monster, hallucination, or verified entity.

6. **The Red Garments & Nine-Day Threshold Lock (`CANON`)**
   - Red garments are central to the case; their exact function and procedure are **unresolved** in the story and must not be turned into fake real-world folklore instructions.
   - The **nine-day threshold** is a fictional rule within the story.
   - **Material & Color Lock**: The red garments must remain **materially red** (dyed fabric/construction under motivated scene light), not washed in constant red scene lighting.

7. **Cultural Responsibility & Epistemic Hygiene Lock (`CANON`)**
   - Never present a fictional DUST rule as verified universal Jamaican religious or cultural practice.
   - Every record must explicitly distinguish:
     1. Established story canon (`CANON`)
     2. Verified cultural or historical information
     3. Character beliefs inside the fiction
     4. Production design proposals (`PROPOSED`)
     5. Unverified interpretations
     6. Deliberately unresolved mysteries

## 2. Governance Status Vocabulary

Every record, prompt, shot, and asset in this repository must carry one of these explicit governance states:

- `PROPOSED`: Generated or suggested by an agent; not yet approved.
- `IN BUILD`: Currently being constructed or revised.
- `REFERENCE NEEDED`: Blocked until a character, location, wardrobe, or prop reference is provided or approved.
- `READY FOR TEST`: Ready for controlled image, video, audio, or continuity testing.
- `TESTED`: Tested against acceptance criteria; results logged.
- `NEEDS REVISION`: Failed one or more continuity, story, visual, or technical checks.
- `APPROVED ASSET`: Explicitly approved by the human creative authority.
- `CANON`: Locked by the human creative authority as part of the story world.
- `SUPERSEDED`: Replaced by a newer approved version.

> **Authority Rule**: An agent may create, organize, test, compare, and recommend, but may never promote any item to `APPROVED ASSET` or `CANON` unless the source record proves human approval.
