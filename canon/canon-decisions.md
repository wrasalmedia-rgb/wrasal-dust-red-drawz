### `decision-010` — Gate B Wood Heuristic Calibration (Neutral vs. Intrinsically Warm Surfaces)
- **Decision ID**: `decision-010` (human review token: `update_heuristic_spec`)
- **Date**: `2026-10-17`
- **Summary**: The Gate B "zero red scene wash" heuristic is split into two tracks. **Track 1**: the absolute `R/G <= 1.35` ceiling applies **only to neutral achromatic reference surfaces** (unbleached paper drawer liner, plaster, unpainted cloth, skin) measured `> 5 cm` from the garment. **Track 2**: dark wood and other intrinsically warm-pigmented materials are exempt from the absolute ceiling and are instead evaluated by an **occluded wood control delta** of `|Δ(R/G)| <= 0.15` between a wood patch in the garment's lit environment and an occluded same-material wood control outside the garment's light/bounce path. Both tracks must be reported for every frame containing both surface classes.
- **Source link**: Human Creative Authority review of `TEST-DUST-C001-001` (`ARENA EXECUTION REPORT 001`), authorised `2026-10-17`; evidence basis recorded in `verification/tests/TEST-DUST-C001-001/color-measurements.json`
- **Approved by**: Human Creative Authority (explicit post-review authorisation)
- **Affected assets**: `verification/first-production-test.md`, `verification/acceptance-criteria.md`, `continuity-red-material-001`, `verif-test-red-drawz-001`, all future Gate B scoring
- **Status**: `CANON` (Verification Method)
- **Anti-gaming note**: The calibration raises no tolerance on the garment itself. `R/G >= 2.2` and `R/B >= 2.5` on lamp-lit fabric folds remain hard floors, and the prohibition on red scene wash is unchanged — only the **measurement method** for intrinsically warm materials is corrected.
