# Locations — WRASAL DUST: THE RED DRAWZ (Case 001)

This ledger governs all spatial environments for `Case 001: The Nine-Day Threshold`.
**Primary Canon Lock (`decision-001`)**: The entirety of Case 001 takes place in **Harlem, New York City, in 2026**. No scenes are set in Jamaica.

## 1. Established Setting Lock & Proposed Sub-Locations

### `loc-harlem-ext-001` — Harlem Street / Brownstone Threshold (NYC, 2026)
- **Location ID**: `loc-harlem-ext-001`
- **Name**: Harlem Residential Street & Brownstone Threshold (New York City, 2026)
- **City / period**: Harlem, New York City, 2026 (`CANON`)
- **Dramatic function**: Establishes the grounded, contemporary urban reality of Harlem in 2026 before any interior grief or supernatural threshold is crossed. Architecture, masonry, ironwork, sodium/LED municipal street lighting, and seasonal atmosphere anchor the viewer in physical reality (`REALITY FIRST. TECHNOLOGY SECOND.`).
- **Visual references**: `REFERENCE NEEDED` — Primary Notion `Location & Environment Register` is not currently shared with the integration. Note: Accessible Notion record `02 DUST / The Boundary at 3:17 AM` (`3f7c034c-1032-802e-97da-eeef87a677d2`) references night boundary atmosphere at 3:17 AM (`/cinematic /nightphoto /closeup /moody`), which may inform exterior/threshold timing pending human confirmation.
- **Lighting rules**: Motivated 2026 NYC night/pre-dawn or late-afternoon architectural light (practical streetlamps, vestibule bulb, window spill); black stays black with real shadow falloff (`WRASAL_LIGHTING_GRAMMAR_v001: controlled_low_key`). Never bathe the street in stylized red or cyberpunk neon.
- **Set dressing notes**: Authentic Harlem brownstone/apartment stoop geometry, worn limestone or brick treads, painted iron railings, contemporary NYC vestibule hardware, subtle lived urban wear.
- **Continuity risks**:
  - **Conflict Warning**: Notion page `WRASAL / WRAYvolution — Cinematic Portfolio Production Blueprint v0.2` (`3f7c034c-1032-8126-923c-f30e39898acc`) lists `DST-01 / Place: Jamaican night exterior with credible architecture, foliage, humidity`. That shot spec conflicts with the Case 001 Harlem 2026 lock (`decision-001`) and must not be used for Case 001.
- **Status**: `CANON` (Harlem NYC 2026 macro-setting) / `PROPOSED` (specific stoop/threshold sub-location) / `REFERENCE NEEDED` (approved location plates)
