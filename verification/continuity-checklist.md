# Continuity checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate whether a generated result or proposed asset remains consistent with the project’s existing world and story logic.

## Checklist

- [ ] Asset ID is present and stable.
- [ ] The asset status is explicitly labeled.
- [ ] The asset is linked to the correct character, location, or prop record.
- [ ] The output does not conflict with approved canon.
- [ ] Visual continuity and narrative continuity are both reviewed.
- [ ] Assumptions or unresolved issues are documented.
- [ ] Human review is recorded before approval.

## Review rule

If a checklist item cannot be confirmed, the result must remain unapproved and the uncertainty must be recorded.
